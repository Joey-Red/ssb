# Pro Lab plan

The controlling long-term Pro Lab completion plan is `docs/PRO_LAB_LONG_TERM_PLAN.md`.

Future work must treat the Pro Lab as incomplete until actual user-facing VOD, reviewed-decision, lesson, matchup, player-comparison, set-breakdown, and training-integration depth is extensive across the 89-fighter roster. Milestone completion is not feature completion.
